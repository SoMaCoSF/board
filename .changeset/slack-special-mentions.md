Slack's special mentions in a board message no longer ping anyone in Slack. `<!channel>`, `<!here>`, `<!everyone>`, and `<!subteam^…>` from a GitHub comment, a WhatsApp message, or an agent's reply post as written, with a word joiner after the `<`, so the app can't be made to notify a whole channel or user group.
