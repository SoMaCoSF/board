A message sync copies to Slack posts under its author's name and picture when the app has `chat:write.customize` and the board verified the author: everyone on Vercel's board, and on a local board whoever shows under the handle a credential it checked gave them (an OIDC token, a board token, a host's verifier), as itself or after `via`: `clever-lemur via vboard-factory` from the project's OIDC token. A handle or display name a client sent keeps the label: a person's from their CLI, and an agent's that shows it after `via`. Actor views say so with `verified: true`. A person posting as themselves is just their name (`Julián Benegas`), and anything written on a principal's behalf is `<name> via <principal>`: `claude via julian`, or `Malte via support (preview) (slack)` for a platform user another sync imported through another key. Its `**name**: ` label is left out. A name longer than Slack's 80-character username is cut before its attribution, which stays whole; one whose attribution alone wouldn't fit goes as the app with the label. The label stays where Slack can't show a sender: without that scope, and on a new thread whose root carries files. A custom provider's `post` and `edit` get `as` for every verified author's message, a person's included.
